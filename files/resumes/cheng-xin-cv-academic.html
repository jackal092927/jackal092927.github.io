Bio

Cheng Xin is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at California State University, Fresno. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Purdue University under the supervision of Dr. Tamal K. Dey and was a postdoctoral researcher at Rutgers University advised by Dr. Jie Gao. His research develops trustworthy and theoretically grounded AI systems by connecting topology, geometry, algorithms, and multi-agent interaction. His work spans trustworthy and multi-agent AI, topological and geometric learning, non-Euclidean representations, 3D and video generative AI, and AI for Science, with publications in NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, and SoCG.
